Tetrar

Claim four. Hold the most.

New Game

How to play

On your turn, claim up to 4 empty cells. Each cell must be orthogonally connected to your existing territory — or to another cell you're claiming this same turn, so you can grow a chain of four.

If you can place fewer than four, place what you can and end your turn. When no one can move, whoever holds the most cells wins.
