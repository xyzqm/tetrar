Claim four. Hold the most.

How to play

On your turn, claim up to 4 empty cells — click or drag across them. Each cell must be orthogonally connected to your existing territory, or to another cell you're claiming this same turn, so you can grow a chain of four.

Seal off empty space with your color and it's captured — board edges count as walls, and trapped opponent cells are left alone but don't prevent capture of the empty space around them.

Before the game, each player secretly places mines 💣. If any cell you select contains a mine, your entire turn is skipped — and you won't know which cell caused it. Probe cautiously or risk losing a turn. Enclose a region to reveal any mines in it and earn bonus points for each one that isn't your own.

If you can place fewer than four, place what you can and end your turn. When no one can move, whoever holds the most cells wins.
