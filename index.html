How to play

On your turn, claim up to 4 empty cells — click or drag across them. Each cell must be orthogonally connected to your existing territory, or to another cell you're claiming this same turn, so you can grow a chain of four.

Seal off empty space with your color and it's captured — the board edges count as walls, so any empty region only your color borders becomes yours. (Trapped opponent cells are left alone.)

If you can place fewer than four, place what you can and end your turn. When no one can move, whoever holds the most cells wins.
